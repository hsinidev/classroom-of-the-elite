About the Project

The Advanced Strategic Terminal

Classroom of the Elite Online is more than a reading platform; it is a meticulously designed data hub for the series' most dedicated analysts. Our mission is to provide high-quality access to both Year 1 and Year 2 manga chapters while maintaining an atmosphere of intellectual rigor.

Our Objective

Borrowing the 'OAA' (Over-All Ability) philosophy from the Advanced Nurturing High School, we focus on three core pillars: Performance, Precision, and Perception. Our terminal is optimized for speed and clarity, mirroring the high-stakes world of Kiyotaka Ayanokouji.

Project Architecture

The site relies on a static-build terminal system, ensuring that your strategic data remains locally accessible and fast-loading. Every module, from the chapter reader to the deep-dive theory guides, is built for maximum tactical advantage.
