Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) Policy

Classroom of the Elite Online (“the Platform”) provides a strategic, academically-oriented venue for readers to access manga titles that are lawfully licensed or otherwise cleared for public distribution. In accordance with 17 U.S.C. § 512 and the principles of user safety, we maintain a rigorous copyright-infringement removal process. The following policy outlines the rights and obligations of copyright holders, registered users, and the Platform itself.

2. Submitting a DMCA Takedown Notice

To file a valid notice, the claimant must provide the following information in writing (email is acceptable) and ensure that each statement is accurate under penalty of perjury:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright holder or a person authorized to act on the holder’s behalf.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work(s) claimed to be infringed, including registration numbers, URLs, or other documentation that evidences ownership.
  • A clear description of the material on Classroom of the Elite Online that is alleged to be infringing, together with a precise URL.
  • A statement that the claimant has a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized.
  • A statement that the information in the notice is accurate.

3. Counter-Notification Procedure

If a user believes that the material was removed in error, they may submit a counter-notice. A valid counter-notice must contain the user's signature, identification of the removed material, and a statement under penalty of perjury that it was a mistake.

4. Strategic Conduct & Record-Keeping

Our community is built upon the analytical spirit exemplified in the source material. We require all participants to respect intellectual-property rights and refrain from uploading unlicensed works. Users found to be abusing the service will be subject to account suspension or termination without prior notice.
